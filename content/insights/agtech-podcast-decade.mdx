We read every transcript of two leading agtech podcasts — 689 episodes and 4.6 million words spanning 2016–2026 — and let the themes emerge from the data rather than imposing categories. Seventeen themes surfaced, and the headline is not a technology: the decade's conversation is dominated by money and the grower relationship, with AI arriving as the defining recent inflection, carbon cresting in 2021–22, and blockchain the cleanest boom-and-bust on record.

This is a fully interactive, chart-driven piece — decade-long theme timelines, the rise and fall of each conversation, and all 3,335 quoted passages shown verbatim with their surrounding transcript context, every one machine-verified against the source transcript.
